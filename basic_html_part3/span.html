The Arc Core Facts

The Arc Core is one of Tony Stark's most remarkable inventions, providing the enormous amount of energy required to power his Iron Man armor. Its compact design allows it to generate tremendous power while fitting inside a relatively small space. The reactor can supply energy to systems such as flight, repulsors, sensors, weapons, and life-support equipment. In addition to powering his armor, Tony originally used a miniature Arc Reactor in his chest to keep dangerous shrapnel away from his heart. Its distinctive blue glow has since become one of the most recognizable symbols of Stark's technology.
